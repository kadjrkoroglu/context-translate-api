Privacy Policy

Context Translate & Flashcards · Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who processes it. In short: we only use your data to run the app's features. We don't show ads, we don't track you across apps, and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account: a user ID, and if you sign in, your email address and name as given by email sign-up, Google or Apple. You can use the app as a guest without an account.
  • Your study content: decks, cards, favorites and translation history. They are stored on your phone and, when you sign in and sync, in our cloud database so you can use them on other devices.
  • Text you translate with AI: the text you enter, and for photo translation the text read from the photo.
  • Voice in Live translation: your speech is streamed to be translated in real time. We don't store recordings.
  • Study with AI: the cards of your current session and your answers, kept on our server so you can resume a session and so session limits work.
  • Usage counts: how many translations, Live minutes and study sessions you have used, to apply your plan's limits.
  • Subscription status once subscriptions are available: whether you have an active plan. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos never leave your phone. The text in a photo is read on the device; only that text is sent for translation.
  • Basic (offline) translation runs entirely on your phone.
  • Dictation uses your phone's speech recognition (Apple), under Apple's privacy terms.

Who processes your data

  • Google Gemini (Google LLC) processes the text, photo text, Live audio and Study with AI answers you send with the AI features. We ask for your permission before the first use. We use the paid Gemini API, under which Google does not use this data to improve its products.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, the cloud database for synced content, and the server that runs our features.
  • Supabase: the database that stores usage counts, plan limits and Study with AI sessions.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions and speech recognition.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, and the offline translation and text recognition models (they run on your phone).

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your account, synced content, usage counts and study sessions are deleted from our systems. Guest data stays on your phone until you delete the app.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time in the app: Profile > Delete account.
  • Use the app without signing in, or without the AI features (Basic translation works without them).
  • Contact us to ask what we hold about you or to have it corrected or deleted.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
